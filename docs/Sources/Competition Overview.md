Overview

Build systems that know when they need attention. Predict failure. Prevent waste.

Extend the life of the systems our world depends on.

Welcome to the Sustain the System competition! Sustainability is at the core of Nordic's technology and innovation. Using Nordic Semiconductor's nRF54LM20 DK and Bluetooth® technology, build a predictive monitoring solution that helps equipment, infrastructure, or natural resources last longer through early detection and proactive maintenance.

Your challenge is to create a system that detects degradation or other issues before they lead to failure, waste, or replacement. The nRF54LM20A/B can run machine learning models that enable edge computing. Examples include:

Equipment

    Bearing wear detection
    Pump health monitoring
    HVAC predictive maintenance
    Conveyor motor monitoring
    Generator monitoring
    Solar inverter health tracking
    Smart irrigation systems
    Agricultural equipment monitoring

Infrastructure

    Community infrastructure monitoring
    Building energy system monitoring
    Greenhouse monitoring

Natural resources

    Smart irrigation systems
    Animal and wildlife health monitoring
    Water resource monitoring

Using Nordic's nRF54LM20 DK, your solution should:

Connect sustainability to practical, real-world outcomes by demonstrating how Bluetooth® technology can:

    Collect and share meaningful data from the physical world
    Monitor changes and detect critical conditions
    Alert users and recommend next steps based on actionable insights
    Improve efficiency, resilience, or resource utilization

nRF54LM20DK info image (1).jpg

The nRF54LM20 DK is the development kit for the nRF54LM20A and nRF54LM20B SoCs. This single-board development kit gives developers full access to the features of these ultra-low-power wireless SoCs — including the nRF54LM20B's integrated Axon NPU — along with support for Bluetooth LE, Bluetooth Channel Sounding, Matter, Thread, Zigbee, and Aliro.
