## Implications for our direction

These are our interpretations of the headings, not judgments of entrants' work.

1. **Machine monitoring has substantial visible overlap.** Motor, pump, bearing, fan, machine-tool, inverter, and acoustic/vibration titles are common. A different asset name alone is a weaker distinction than a useful operating policy supported by measurements.
2. **Operating-state awareness is already claimed.** Operating-point-aware pump diagnostics and Condition-Aware Physical AI are visible. Demonstrate separation of normal speed/load changes from deterioration rather than claim the concept alone is new.
3. **Distributed sensing needs sharper evidence.** Shop Sentinel tags, AgriGuard EQ mesh, Public Asset Health Mesh, and bluMesh-AI are adjacent network-oriented ideas. Their topology, adaptive policies, and measured power remain unknown. We cannot establish uniqueness from headings.
4. **Bluetooth has an explicit Channel Sounding candidate.** SafeRange presents a plausible Bluetooth/Edge AI angle, but its results and maintenance relevance are unestablished. Its presence does not require changing our adaptive-energy direction.
5. **Sensor fusion is visible.** BuildingDNA names vibration and current, while the industrial stethoscope names acoustics and vibration. Show why our selected features improve an actionable maintenance decision.
6. **Seal and fouling topics are not empty territory.** FridgeSeal, the self-diagnosing nephelometer, and ResinGuard name adjacent problems. No current heading explicitly names compressed-air leakage, valve stiction, or membrane fouling, but vague names and unavailable details prevent an exclusivity claim.
7. **Select a measurable intervention.** Favor an accessible degradation mechanism with realistic ground truth, fair fixed-rate baselines, and measurable resource benefit over an unusual name. No use case or award category is selected by this research.
